Privacy Policy

AI InsureGenie ("we") helps people compare auto insurance options. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

The answers you give in the chat (such as ZIP code, vehicle, and driving record), and, if you choose to continue, your first name, phone number, and email address. We also record technical details such as your IP address and browser type, and the date and wording of the consent you give.

How we use it

To show you insurance options and, with your consent, to share your details with the insurance companies and agencies we list so they can contact you about quotes. We may contact you about your request.

Who we share it with

Only the insurance partners named in the consent box you accept, and service providers that help us run the site. We do not sell your information for any other purpose.
