Partner FAQ

Questions from partners

Can I target specific states or drivers?

Yes. You can limit your placement by state and by profile, such as coverage level, driving record, or whether the driver is insured today.

How do you handle consent and disclosure?

Before a driver continues to you, the assistant names your brand and explains why it recommends you. Drivers can always choose not to continue. We don't pass personal details without the driver's agreement.

Where does your traffic come from?

Drivers find us through search, our car insurance guides, and other channels. We're happy to share the source mix for your placement.
