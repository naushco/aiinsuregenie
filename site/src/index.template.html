FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Ask the assistant.

Is AI InsureGenie free to use?

Yes. Chatting with the assistant and getting matched is free, and you never have to buy a policy.

How does AI InsureGenie work?

You tell the assistant what matters to you, such as price or coverage, plus a few details about you and your car. It recommends the quoting partner that fits, and you get your quote on that partner's website.

How does AI InsureGenie make money?

Our quoting partners may pay us a fee when you visit them or request a quote. It costs you nothing, and the assistant always tells you which partner it recommends and why.

Do I still need to fill in a form?

Yes, to see real quotes you fill in the partner's form on their website. The chat helps you pick the right partner first, so you don't fill in forms on several sites.

What affects the price of my car insurance?

Common factors include where you live, your vehicle, your driving record, how much you drive, the coverage and deductible you choose, and in many places your claims history.
