Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Ask Genie.

Is AI InsureGenie free?

Yes. Chatting with Genie and getting matched is free, and you never have to buy a policy. Partners may pay us when you connect with them, and that doesn't change the price they quote you. How we make money.

Will I get spam calls?

No. Chatting with Genie never puts your number on a list. You only hear from the partner you choose, and only after you agree.

Is Genie a licensed insurance agent?

No. Genie is an AI assistant. It points you to licensed quoting partners, and your quotes and policy come from them or the insurer.

Who are your partners?

Licensed quoting platforms, agencies and insurers. Partners are always labeled, and they may pay us a fee when you visit them or request a quote.

Can I get insurance with a DUI or if I need an SR-22?

Usually yes, though fewer insurers file SR-22s and prices are often higher. Genie points you to partners that file SR-22s in your state. See our SR-22 guide.

Does checking my options affect my credit?

Chatting with Genie doesn't check your credit. When you request quotes, insurers in many states may use a credit-based insurance score, which is usually a soft check that doesn't lower your credit score. Ask the partner if you're unsure.

Do I still need to fill in a form?

Yes, to see real quotes you fill in one form with the partner you pick. Genie helps you pick the right partner first, so you don't fill in forms on several sites.
