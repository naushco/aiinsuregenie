Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

You can tell us not to sell or share your personal information with our insurance partners. It's free, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

When you agree to be contacted, we may share your details with insurance partners who pay us. Under some state privacy laws, such as California's, that can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Send this form and we will stop sharing the details you give us with partners.

Your browser is sending a Global Privacy Control signal. We treat it as a request not to sell or share, so the chat on this device already marks anything you send as do-not-share.

Enter at least one. We only use it to find and flag your records.

What happens next

  • New requests from this email or phone are kept but not sent to partners.
  • If we already shared your details, we'll tell those partners to stop using them.
  • You can still use the chat and visit partners yourself.

To see, correct, or delete your information, or for any other privacy request, use our Contact page. See our Privacy policy for details.
