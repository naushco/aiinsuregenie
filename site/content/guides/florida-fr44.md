Florida uses an **FR-44** instead of an SR-22 for its most serious driving offenses, mainly DUI. It works the same way, with your insurer filing proof of coverage with the state, but it requires much higher liability limits. Most drivers need it for about **three years**.

[[genie]]

## Who needs an FR-44 in Florida

You're usually required to file an FR-44 after:

- A DUI conviction
- Hit-and-run, or fleeing police after a traffic violation
- Vehicular manslaughter
- Driving on a suspended or revoked license for the third time

Less serious problems, like driving without insurance, usually call for an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/) instead. Virginia also uses FR-44s, with limits at double its normal minimums.

## FR-44 coverage limits in Florida

| Coverage | FR-44 minimum | Regular SR-22 minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bodily injury, one person | $100,000 | $10,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $300,000 | $20,000 |
| Property damage | $50,000 | $10,000 |
| Personal injury protection (PIP) | $10,000 | $10,000 |

The FR-44 limits are many times higher than Florida's usual minimums. That is the main reason FR-44 insurance costs more.

## How long you need it

Typically **three years**, counted from when your license was suspended, with no gaps. If your policy lapses, the insurer tells the state and your license can be suspended again.

## What it costs

- **Filing fee:** often about $15 to $25.
- **Reinstatement fee:** often about $150 to $500 after a DUI, paid to the state.
- **Premium:** one 2024 analysis found about **$1,309 a year** for minimum FR-44 coverage after a DUI, but prices vary widely by insurer. Some insurers ask you to pay several months up front.

## How to get an FR-44

1. Confirm in your court or FLHSMV paperwork that you need an FR-44, not an SR-22.
2. Find an insurer that files FR-44s in Florida. Not every insurer does. Genie can point you to a quoting partner.
3. Buy at least 100/300/50 plus $10,000 PIP and ask the insurer to file the FR-44.
4. Pay the state's reinstatement fee and finish any other steps on your notice.
5. Keep the policy active for the full period.

## Common questions

### Is an FR-44 the same as an SR-22?
They do the same job, proving you carry insurance, but an FR-44 requires much higher liability limits. Florida and Virginia are the states that use FR-44s.

### Can I get an FR-44 if I don't own a car?
Often, yes, through a non-owner policy that meets the FR-44 limits. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/) for how non-owner policies work.

### Why is FR-44 insurance so expensive?
Partly the DUI on your record, and partly the higher required limits. Comparing several insurers is the best way to lower the price.
