**Quick answer:** an FR-44 is a form your insurer files with Florida to prove you carry extra-high liability insurance after a DUI. You need at least **$100,000/$300,000/$50,000** in liability coverage, and you must keep it for **three years** ([FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs)).

In Florida, drivers convicted of DUI must carry much higher liability limits and prove it with an **FR-44** form, filed by their insurer. Florida's motor vehicle department says you must keep the FR-44 for **three years from the date your driving privilege is reinstated** ([FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs)).

## Who needs an FR-44 in Florida

Florida law requires the higher FR-44 limits for drivers convicted of DUI (or who pleaded guilty or no contest) on or after October 1, 2007 ([Florida Statutes s. 324.023](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2011/324.023); [FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs)). Some other serious offenses can also lead to an FR-44 requirement, so check your notice ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance)).

Less serious problems, like driving without insurance, usually call for an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/) instead ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance)). Virginia also uses FR-44s, mainly after DUI convictions ([CarInsurance.com](https://carinsurance.com/fr-44-insurance-virginia.aspx)).

## FR-44 coverage limits in Florida

| Coverage | FR-44 minimum | Florida SR-22 minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bodily injury, one person | $100,000 | $10,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $300,000 | $20,000 |
| Property damage | $50,000 | $10,000 |

FR-44 limits come from [FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs) and [s. 324.023](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2011/324.023). SR-22 minimums are from [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance), which also notes $10,000 of personal injury protection (PIP).

## How long you need it

**Three years** from the date your driving privilege is reinstated ([FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs)), with no gaps in coverage. Florida insurers must report cancellations to the state, which can then suspend your license and registration ([Florida Statutes s. 324.0221](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/324.0221)).

## What it costs

- **Filing fee:** often about $15 to $25 ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance)).
- **Premium:** estimates vary by study. One 2026 analysis put the average FR-44 driver in Florida at about **$167 a month for liability only** and **$274 a month for full coverage**, compared with $143 and $235 for a driver with a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/florida/sr-22-car-insurance/)). An earlier analysis found about **$1,309 a year** for minimum FR-44 coverage after a DUI ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance)).

Prices vary a lot by insurer. In the 2026 study, the lowest monthly averages for FR-44 drivers were ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/florida/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| State Farm | $94 | $146 |
| Progressive | $105 | $166 |
| GEICO | $107 | not listed |
| Allstate | not listed | $202 |

These are estimates for a sample driver. Your quote depends on your record, car, ZIP code and insurer. Some insurers ask for several months up front.

## How to get an FR-44

1. Confirm in your court or FLHSMV paperwork that you need an FR-44, not an SR-22.
2. Find an insurer that files FR-44s in Florida. Not every insurer does. Genie can point you to a quoting partner.
3. Buy at least 100/300/50 coverage and ask the insurer to file the FR-44.
4. Finish the other reinstatement steps on your notice.
5. Keep the policy active for the full three years.

## Common questions

### What is an FR-44?
An FR-44 is a certificate your insurance company files with the state to show you carry the higher liability limits required after a DUI. It is not a type of policy itself. Florida requires it for three years from reinstatement ([FLHSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/driver-licenses-id-cards/education-courses/dui-and-iid/dui-faqs)).

### Which states use FR-44 insurance?
Only Florida and [Virginia](/fr44-insurance/virginia/) use FR-44 filings. Other states use an SR-22 instead ([Progressive](https://www.progressive.com/answers/fr-44/)).

### What happens if my FR-44 policy lapses or is cancelled?
Your insurer must report the cancellation to the state, which can suspend your license and registration until you get covered again ([Florida Statutes s. 324.0221](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/324.0221)). Keep payments current for the full three years.

### Is an FR-44 the same as an SR-22?
They do the same job, proving you carry insurance, but an FR-44 requires much higher liability limits ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/insurance/fr44-insurance)).

### Can I get an FR-44 if I don't own a car?
Often, yes, through a non-owner policy that meets the FR-44 limits. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/) for how non-owner policies work.

### Why is FR-44 insurance so expensive?
Partly the DUI on your record, and partly the much higher required limits. Comparing several insurers is the best way to lower the price.
