**Quick answer:** a good student discount can cut car insurance for a young driver by **up to 25%**, usually for a full-time student with a **B average (3.0 GPA) or better** ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/discounts/good-student-discount/)). State Farm offers up to 25%, GEICO 15%, Progressive about 10% on average and USAA up to 10%. For teens, whose rates are the highest of any driver, it's one of the biggest discounts available.

## Good student discount by insurer

Per [Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/discounts/good-student-discount/):

| Insurer | Discount | Main requirements |
|---|---|---|
| State Farm | Up to 25% | Varies by state, often a B average / 3.0 GPA or top 20% of class |
| GEICO | 15% | Full-time student with a B average or better |
| Progressive | About 10% on average | Full-time, under 23, B average, on a family policy |
| USAA | Up to 10% | Full-time, under 25, B average, 3.0 GPA, top 20% or dean's list |
| Allstate | Not published | Under 25, unmarried, 2.7 GPA or other options |

American Family, Farmers, Nationwide and Travelers also offer it, with rules that vary by state.

## Who qualifies

Most insurers require ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/discounts/good-student-discount/)):

- **Full-time** high school or college enrollment
- A **B average or 3.0 GPA** (Allstate accepts 2.7), or a top-20% class rank, honor roll or dean's list
- An **age limit**, typically under 23 to 25 depending on the insurer

Some insurers accept standardized test scores for homeschooled students.

## How to get it

1. **Ask your insurer** before or when you add your teen. It's often not applied automatically.
2. **Send proof:** a recent report card or transcript, or a form signed by the school.
3. **Renew it:** insurers may ask for updated grades at renewal.

## Stack it with other discounts

Combine it with driver's education, safe-driving apps and multi-car discounts. Students at school far from home without a car may also pay less. Allstate's version requires being 100+ miles from where the car is kept. ## Common questions

### How much is the good student discount?
Up to 25% at some insurers. GEICO gives 15%, Progressive about 10% on average and USAA up to 10% ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/discounts/good-student-discount/)).

### What GPA do you need for the good student discount?
Usually 3.0 (a B average). Allstate accepts 2.7 (same source).

### How long does the good student discount last?
Until the driver passes the insurer's age limit (often 23 to 25) or stops meeting the grade rules.

### Does the good student discount apply to college students?
Yes, at most insurers, if they're full-time and meet the grade and age rules.
