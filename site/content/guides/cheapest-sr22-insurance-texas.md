**Quick answer:** in one 2026 analysis, the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Texas came from **State Farm at about $51 a month** for liability only, followed by Progressive ($68), Mile Auto ($71) and GEICO ($74). The average Texas driver with an SR-22 paid about **$122 a month for liability** and **$226 for full coverage**, compared with $110 and $204 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/texas/sr-22-car-insurance)). Prices vary a lot by company, so compare several.

## If your SR-22 is for a DUI

A DUI raises prices more than most other reasons for an SR-22. Average monthly full-coverage prices for Texas drivers with a DUI ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/auto-insurance/texas/companies)):

| Insurer | Full coverage, monthly |
|---|---|
| State Farm | $142 |
| USAA (military families only) | $239 |
| GEICO | $317 |
| Progressive | $324 |
| AAA | $519 |

For comparison, the average Texas driver pays about $247 a month for full coverage and $87 for minimum liability (same source).

## What the SR-22 itself costs

The filing fee is usually a one-time **$15 to $50**. The real cost is the higher premium, which you'll pay for the **two years** Texas usually requires the SR-22 ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/texas/sr-22-car-insurance)). Your SR-22 must meet Texas's minimum 30/60/25 liability limits. See our [Texas SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/texas/) for the full rules.

## How to pay less for SR-22 insurance in Texas

1. **Compare at least 3 to 5 insurers.** In the table above, the same driver's liability price ranges from $51 to $96 a month.
2. **Choose liability only if your car is older and paid off.** Full coverage roughly doubles the price.
3. **Don't let it lapse.** If your SR-22 policy is cancelled, your insurer tells the state and your license can be suspended again. Set up autopay.
4. **Consider a non-owner SR-22** if you don't own a car. It's usually cheaper. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
5. **Ask about discounts:** paying in full, defensive driving courses, bundling and paperless billing.

## Common questions

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Texas?
In one 2026 study, State Farm was cheapest at about $51 a month for liability and $97 for full coverage ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/texas/sr-22-car-insurance)).

### How much is SR-22 insurance in Texas per month?
On average about $122 a month for liability only and $226 for full coverage (same source). After a DUI, full coverage averages more, from about $142 to $519 a month depending on the insurer ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/auto-insurance/texas/companies)).

### How much does it cost to file an SR-22 in Texas?
Usually a one-time fee of $15 to $50, charged by the insurer.

### How long do I need an SR-22 in Texas?
Usually two years. See our [Texas SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/texas/) for when the clock starts.

### Can I get a cheaper SR-22 without a car?
Yes. A non-owner SR-22 policy is usually cheaper because it doesn't insure a vehicle.
