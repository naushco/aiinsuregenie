**Quick answer:** a Virginia FR-44 is a certificate your insurer files with the DMV to prove you carry **double the state's minimum liability coverage** after a DUI-related conviction ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)). For policies starting in 2025 or later, that means **$100,000/$200,000/$50,000**. You must keep it on file for **three years**.

[[genie]]

## Who needs an FR-44 in Virginia

The Virginia DMV requires an FR-44 when your license is suspended or revoked for a DUI-related conviction, including ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)):

- Driving under the influence (Va. Code § 18.2-266)
- Maiming while driving under the influence (§ 18.2-51.4)
- Driving while suspended or revoked for a DUI-related offense (§ 18.2-272)

Other suspensions, such as an unpaid judgment or driving uninsured, need an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/) instead, with standard limits.

## FR-44 coverage limits in Virginia

An FR-44 policy must carry double Virginia's minimum liability limits ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)). Virginia raised its minimums for policies effective on or after January 1, 2025 ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/vehicles/insurance-requirements)):

| Coverage | Virginia minimum (2025+) | FR-44 (double) |
|---|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $50,000 | $100,000 |
| Bodily injury, two or more people | $100,000 | $200,000 |
| Property damage | $25,000 | $50,000 |

Older DMV documents may still show lower limits from before 2025. Ask your insurer to confirm the limits on your filing.

## How long you need it

The certificate must stay on file with the DMV ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)):

- to get restricted driving privileges, and for as long as you have them, and
- for **three years** from the end date of your suspension or revocation.

If your policy is cancelled during those three years, your insurer notifies the DMV, which suspends or revokes your driving privilege until a new FR-44 is filed. Keep payments current. Our guide to [late car insurance payments](/late-car-insurance-payment/) explains how grace periods work.

## What FR-44 insurance costs in Virginia

One 2026 analysis found an average of about **$337 a month** for full coverage with an FR-44 after one DUI, compared with about **$169 a month** for full coverage in Virginia overall ([ValuePenguin](https://valuepenguin.com/car-insurance/sr22-fr44-virginia)). Prices vary a lot by insurer, so compare. In that study, the lowest averages for a driver with a DUI were:

| Insurer | Average monthly, full coverage |
|---|---|
| Travelers | $196 |
| USAA (military families only) | $259 |
| Erie | $277 |
| Auto-Owners | $289 |
| Progressive | $320 |

Insurers may also charge a small filing fee.

## FR-44 if you don't own a car

You still have to file. If you don't own a vehicle, the DMV requires an **operator's policy**, which covers you as a driver of other cars but doesn't insure a vehicle ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)). See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/) for how non-owner policies work.

## How to get an FR-44 in Virginia

1. **Find an insurer that files FR-44s.** Not every company does, especially after a DUI.
2. **Buy a policy with at least the FR-44 limits** above.
3. **Your insurer files it with the DMV electronically.** You don't file it yourself ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)).
4. **Complete the DMV's other reinstatement steps,** such as fees. Check your compliance summary at dmv.virginia.gov.

## Common questions

### What are the FR-44 limits in Virginia?
Double the state minimum: $100,000 per person, $200,000 per accident and $50,000 property damage for policies from 2025 on ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/vehicles/insurance-requirements)).

### How long do you need an FR-44 in Virginia?
Three years from the end of your suspension or revocation, plus any time you hold restricted driving privileges ([Virginia DMV](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/dmv292.pdf)).

### Is an FR-44 the same as an SR-22 in Virginia?
Both prove insurance, but an FR-44 is for DUI-related convictions and requires double the minimum limits. An SR-22 uses the standard minimums.

### Which states use FR-44?
Only Virginia and [Florida](/fr44-insurance/florida/). Other states use an SR-22 ([Progressive](https://www.progressive.com/answers/fr-44/)).

### How much does FR-44 insurance cost in Virginia?
About $337 a month on average for full coverage after one DUI in one 2026 study, with some insurers far cheaper ([ValuePenguin](https://valuepenguin.com/car-insurance/sr22-fr44-virginia)).
