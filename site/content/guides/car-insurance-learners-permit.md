**Quick answer:** yes, a driver with a learner's permit must be covered by car insurance, but most don't need their own policy. In many cases a permit driver is covered under a parent's or guardian's policy, and adding a permit driver usually **doesn't raise the price until they get a license** ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-permit-drivers-need-insurance/)). Call your insurer before your teen starts driving, because each company sets its own rules.

[[genie]]

## Do permit drivers need car insurance?

In every state, the car a permit driver uses must carry at least the state's minimum insurance ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-permit-drivers-need-insurance/)). New drivers always need coverage, including while they're learning ([Progressive](https://progressive.com/answers/teen-drivers-learners-permit-insurance/)). What differs is **whose policy** covers them:

- **Teen living with a parent:** usually covered through the parent's policy.
- **Adult learner, or someone without an insured parent at home:** usually needs their own policy.
- **Permit holder who owns a car:** can buy a policy for that car.

## Does a parent's policy cover a permit driver?

Often, yes. Some insurers cover a permit driver automatically until they're licensed. Others want them listed on the policy as soon as they get the permit, especially if the teen is already old enough to be licensed ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-permit-drivers-need-insurance/)).

That's why the Insurance Information Institute advises calling your insurance professional **before** your teen gets a learner's permit ([Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/article/auto-insurance-for-teen-drivers)). In Texas, for example, you aren't legally required to add a teen who only has a permit, but you should tell your insurer. Not telling them could lead to a denied claim or a cancelled policy ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)).

## Will my rates go up for a permit driver?

Usually not yet. Adding a permit driver typically doesn't raise the premium. The increase comes when the teen is **licensed**, when adding them can raise a parent's premium by 50% to 100% ([Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/article/background-on-teen-drivers)). Use the permit months to plan: ask your insurer for a quote with your teen licensed, and line up discounts like good student and driver's ed. See [what young drivers pay by age](/new-driver-car-insurance/).

## How graduated licensing works

Most states license teens in three stages, typically for drivers aged 15 to 18 ([Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/article/background-on-teen-drivers)):

1. **Learner's permit:** vision and knowledge tests, supervised driving only, seat belts for everyone, zero alcohol.
2. **Intermediate license:** a road test, then limits such as fewer teen passengers or no late-night driving.
3. **Full license:** after the intermediate stage with a clean record.

Your insurer cares most about stages 2 and 3, when your teen can drive alone.

## Do you need insurance for the driving test?

Generally, yes. The car used for the road test needs insurance, though it can come from the owner's policy rather than your own, and you may need to show proof for that car ([Experian](https://experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-you-need-car-insurance-for-driving-test)). Check your state DMV's website for exactly what to bring, and make sure the policy is current before test day.

## Adult learners

If you're an adult with a permit and no insured household car, you'll usually need your own policy, or must practice in a car whose owner's policy covers you. Ask the insurer directly, since some companies won't write a policy for a driver without a full license.

## Common questions

### Can you get car insurance with a learner's permit?
Yes. Most teens are covered through a parent's policy. A permit holder who is an adult, doesn't live with an insured parent, or owns a car can usually buy their own policy ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-permit-drivers-need-insurance/)).

### Do I have to add my permit driver to my insurance?
It depends on your insurer. Some cover permit drivers automatically, others require listing them. Call before your teen starts driving ([Insurance Information Institute](https://www.iii.org/article/auto-insurance-for-teen-drivers)).

### Does car insurance go up with a permit driver?
Usually not until the teen is licensed ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/do-permit-drivers-need-insurance/)).

### Is a minor with a permit allowed their own policy?
Rarely. Minors generally can't buy their own policy, so a parent or guardian adds them ([The Zebra](https://www.thezebra.com/resources/car-insurance/teen-driving-report/)).

### Do I need insurance to learn to drive?
The car you learn in must be insured, and the policy needs to cover you as a driver. For most teens, that's a parent's policy.
