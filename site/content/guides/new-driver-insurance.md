New and young drivers usually pay more for car insurance because they have little driving history. The good news: discounts for good grades, driver's education and safe driving can lower the price, and the car you choose matters too ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)).

[[genie]]

## Do I have to add my teen to my policy?

If a new teen driver lives with you, they need to be insured ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)). Insurers also expect every driver in the household to be listed. Leaving one out can lead an insurer to cancel or not renew your policy if they later have an accident ([NAIC](https://content.naic.org/article/consumer-insight-comparing-online-auto-insurance-quotes)).

## What about a learner's permit?

In Texas, you aren't legally required to insure a teen who only has a learner's permit, but you should **tell your insurer** when a teen is learning in your car. Not telling them could lead to a denied claim or a cancelled policy ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)). Rules vary by state and insurer, so ask yours.

## Discounts that lower the price

Per the [Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html):

- **Good grades:** teens with good grades or test scores can get discounts, which vary by company.
- **Driver's education:** teens who finish a course usually get a discount.
- **The right car:** an inexpensive car, or one with a good safety rating, usually costs less to insure.
- **Driving apps or devices:** tracking programs usually earn a discount, if the teen drives safely.

## New driver, not a teen?

If you're an adult getting your first policy, the same rule applies: little history usually means a higher price at first. Compare several insurers, ask about every discount, and keep a clean record. Each year without claims or tickets helps.

## Common questions

### Is it cheaper to add my teen to my policy or get them their own?
It depends on the insurer and your situation, so ask for quotes both ways. Prices vary a lot between insurers.

### When should I tell my insurer about my teen?
As soon as they start learning to drive in your car, even with a permit ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)).

### Which discount helps most?
It depends on the insurer. Good-student and driver's-ed discounts are common and can last for years ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/adding-teen-driver-to-insurance-policy.html)).
