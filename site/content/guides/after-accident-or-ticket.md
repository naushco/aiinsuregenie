A ticket or an accident usually raises your car insurance at your next renewal, but how much depends a lot on the insurer. One analysis found average increases of about **34% after a speeding ticket** and **53% after an at-fault accident** ([Insurance.com](https://www.insurance.com/auto-insurance/saving-money/effects-of-traffic-tickets.html)). Comparing insurers is the best way to soften the hit.

[[genie]]

## How much rates usually go up

Average increases for a sample driver ([Insurance.com](https://www.insurance.com/auto-insurance/saving-money/effects-of-traffic-tickets.html)):

| Violation | Average increase |
| --- | --- |
| Speeding 11–29 mph over | about 34% |
| Speeding 30+ mph over | about 39% |
| At-fault accident (over $2,000 damage) | about 53% |
| Reckless driving | about 82% |
| First DUI | about 90% |

These are averages for one sample driver profile. Your increase depends on your state, record and insurer. The increase usually shows up when your policy renews, not right away ([Insurance.com](https://www.insurance.com/auto-insurance/saving-money/effects-of-traffic-tickets.html)).

## How long it affects your price

Insurers typically raise premiums for at least **three years** after an at-fault accident, depending on how serious it was and your state's rules ([Insurify, citing Triple-I](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)). A not-at-fault accident can raise rates too, but usually by less ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).

## Ways to pay less

1. **Compare several insurers.** Increases vary a lot by company, so get four or five quotes ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).
2. **Ask about a defensive driving course.** It may earn a discount that offsets part of the increase ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).
3. **Consider a higher deductible,** if you could afford it after a claim ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).
4. **Look into accident forgiveness for the future.** Some insurers offer it, but it usually has to be on your policy before an accident ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).
5. **Ask about every discount,** such as paid-in-full, bundling or safe-driving apps ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/car-insurance.html)).

If a serious violation led to a license suspension, you may also need an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/).

## Common questions

### Will one speeding ticket raise my insurance?
Often yes, at your next renewal. One analysis found an average increase of about 34% for speeding 11–29 mph over the limit ([Insurance.com](https://www.insurance.com/auto-insurance/saving-money/effects-of-traffic-tickets.html)), though some insurers charge much less.

### Does an accident that wasn't my fault raise my rate?
It can, but the increase is usually smaller than for an at-fault accident ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/driver/driving-record/after-accident/)).

### Should I switch insurers after a ticket?
It's worth comparing. Because insurers weigh tickets and accidents differently, another company may charge you less.
