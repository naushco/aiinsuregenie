**Quick answer:** an SR-22 in Arizona is a form your insurer files with the state MVD to prove you carry at least Arizona's minimum liability coverage (25/50/15). You usually need it for three years. With an SR-22, the average Arizona driver pays about **$120 a month for liability** and **$214 for full coverage**, compared with $91 and $162 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/arizona/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

## Who needs an SR-22 in Arizona

You'll typically be told to file one after ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/arizona/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

- A DUI or other alcohol- or drug-related conviction
- Refusing a breath or blood test
- Driving without insurance or not showing proof
- An unpaid court judgment from a crash

The court or MVD notice tells you if you need one and for how long. See our [complete SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/) for how SR-22s work in general.

## Minimum coverage with an SR-22 in Arizona

Your policy must carry at least Arizona's minimum liability limits:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $25,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $50,000 |
| Property damage | $15,000 |


## How long you need it

Usually **three years** after a license or registration suspension. You must keep coverage active for the whole period, with no gaps.

## What SR-22 insurance costs in Arizona

The SR-22 filing fee is set by your insurer. The bigger cost is the higher premium. Lowest average monthly prices for Arizona drivers with an SR-22 in a 2026 analysis ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/arizona/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-Owners | $64 | $110 |
| Just Pay-Per-Mile | $65 | $131 |
| Mile Auto | $79 | $148 |
| State Farm | $79 | $135 |
| Sun Coast | $83 | $151 |
| Progressive | $86 | $142 |

These are averages for sample drivers, not quotes. Your price depends on why you need the SR-22, your record, car and ZIP code, so compare several insurers.

## If you don't own a car

The MVD says you can get a non-owner SR-22 if you need to meet the requirement but don't own a car ([Arizona MVD](https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/driver-services/license-reinstatement/future-financial-responsibility)). See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).

## If your SR-22 lapses

If you let it lapse, your license and registration are suspended again ([Arizona MVD](https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/driver-services/license-reinstatement/future-financial-responsibility)). The SR-22 period restarts, and you pay a suspension fee plus a new filing. Set up autopay, and see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/) if money is tight.

## Common questions

### How much is SR-22 insurance in Arizona?
About $120 a month for liability and $214 for full coverage on average, with the cheapest insurers from about $64 a month for liability ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/arizona/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### How long do you need an SR-22 in Arizona?
Usually **three years** after a license or registration suspension.

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Arizona?
In one 2026 study, Auto-Owners ($64 a month for liability), Just Pay-Per-Mile ($65) and Mile Auto ($79) were cheapest ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/arizona/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### Can I get an SR-22 in Arizona without a car?
Yes, usually with a non-owner policy that includes the SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
