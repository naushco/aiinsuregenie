**Quick answer:** an SR-22 in Georgia is a form your insurer files with the state DDS to prove you carry at least Georgia's minimum liability coverage (25/50/25). You usually need it for three years. With an SR-22, the average Georgia driver pays about **$194 a month for liability** and **$319 for full coverage**, compared with $157 and $259 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/georgia/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

## Who needs an SR-22 in Georgia

You'll typically be told to file one after ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/georgia/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

- A serious violation such as a DUI
- Repeat convictions for driving without insurance (these need an **SR-22A**)
- Causing an accident while uninsured

The court or DDS notice tells you if you need one and for how long. See our [complete SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/) for how SR-22s work in general.

## Minimum coverage with an SR-22 in Georgia

Your policy must carry at least Georgia's minimum liability limits:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $25,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $50,000 |
| Property damage | $25,000 |

Georgia uses a special **SR-22A** for repeat no-insurance offenses. An SR-22 is accepted only when marked "Paid In Full" ([Georgia DDS](https://dds.georgia.gov/no-proof-insurance-multiple)). You must also serve a 90-day suspension before you can reinstate.

## How long you need it

**Three years** of continuous coverage from the conviction date. You must keep coverage active for the whole period, with no gaps.

## What SR-22 insurance costs in Georgia

The SR-22 filing fee is $15 to $50. The bigger cost is the higher premium. Lowest average monthly prices for Georgia drivers with an SR-22 in a 2026 analysis ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/georgia/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-Owners | $79 | $142 |
| State Farm | $85 | $152 |
| Country Financial | $86 | $154 |
| Mile Auto | $91 | $165 |
| Progressive | $95 | $189 |
| USAA (military families only) | $99 | $178 |

These are averages for sample drivers, not quotes. Your price depends on why you need the SR-22, your record, car and ZIP code, so compare several insurers.

## If you don't own a car

If you don't own a vehicle, Georgia still requires coverage: you must buy an **SR-22A non-owner policy** for three years from the conviction date, paid in full every six months ([Georgia DDS](https://dds.georgia.gov/no-proof-insurance-multiple)). See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).

## If your SR-22 lapses

If an SR-22A policy is cancelled during the three years, the insurer reports it and DDS cancels your license ([Georgia DDS](https://dds.georgia.gov/no-proof-insurance-multiple)). Set up autopay, and see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/) if money is tight.

## Common questions

### How much is SR-22 insurance in Georgia?
About $194 a month for liability and $319 for full coverage on average, with the cheapest insurers from about $79 a month for liability ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/georgia/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### How long do you need an SR-22 in Georgia?
**Three years** of continuous coverage from the conviction date.

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Georgia?
In one 2026 study, Auto-Owners ($79 a month for liability), State Farm ($85) and Country Financial ($86) were cheapest ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/georgia/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### Can I get an SR-22 in Georgia without a car?
Yes, usually with a non-owner policy that includes the SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
