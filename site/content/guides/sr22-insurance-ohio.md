**Quick answer:** an SR-22 in Ohio is a form your insurer files with the state BMV to prove you carry at least Ohio's minimum liability coverage (25/50/25). You usually need it for one year for offenses since April 9, 2025 (three to five years for older offenses). With an SR-22, the average Ohio driver pays about **$84 a month for liability** and **$154 for full coverage**, compared with $64 and $117 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/ohio/sr-22-car-insurance)).

## Who needs an SR-22 in Ohio

You'll typically be told to file one after ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/ohio/sr-22-car-insurance)):

- Driving without insurance or not showing proof after a stop or crash (a "non-compliance" suspension)
- Building up 12 or more points within two years
- An at-fault accident without proof of insurance

The court or BMV notice tells you if you need one and for how long. See our [complete SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/) for how SR-22s work in general.

## Minimum coverage with an SR-22 in Ohio

Your policy must carry at least Ohio's minimum liability limits:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $25,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $50,000 |
| Property damage | $25,000 |


## How long you need it

**One year** for non-compliance suspensions added on or after April 9, 2025. Offenses added earlier carry **three years** for a first offense and **five years** for repeat offenses ([Ohio BMV](https://bmv.ohio.gov/susp-ins-noncomply.aspx)). You must keep coverage active for the whole period, with no gaps.

## What SR-22 insurance costs in Ohio

The SR-22 filing fee is usually about $25. The bigger cost is the higher premium. Lowest average monthly prices for Ohio drivers with an SR-22 in a 2026 analysis ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/ohio/sr-22-car-insurance)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| State Farm | $46 | $76 |
| Mile Auto | $47 | $76 |
| Auto-Owners | $54 | $88 |
| Erie | $54 | $88 |
| Progressive | $54 | $101 |
| GEICO | $61 | $104 |

These are averages for sample drivers, not quotes. Your price depends on why you need the SR-22, your record, car and ZIP code, so compare several insurers.

## If you don't own a car

If you don't own a car, ask insurers for a non-owner policy with an SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).

## If your SR-22 lapses

If your policy lapses or is cancelled, your insurer reports it to the BMV and your license can be suspended again. Set up autopay, and see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/) if money is tight.

## Common questions

### How much is SR-22 insurance in Ohio?
About $84 a month for liability and $154 for full coverage on average, with the cheapest insurers from about $46 a month for liability ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/ohio/sr-22-car-insurance)).

### How long do you need an SR-22 in Ohio?
**One year** for non-compliance suspensions added on or after April 9, 2025. Offenses added earlier carry **three years** for a first offense and **five years** for repeat offenses.

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Ohio?
In one 2026 study, State Farm ($46 a month for liability), Mile Auto ($47) and Auto-Owners ($54) were cheapest ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/ohio/sr-22-car-insurance)).

### Can I get an SR-22 in Ohio without a car?
Yes, usually with a non-owner policy that includes the SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
