**Quick answer:** the fastest way to lower your car insurance after a rate increase is to **compare quotes**. Drivers who switch save about **$694 a year** on average ([CarInsurance.com](https://www.carinsurance.com/how-to-lower-car-insurance)). Then check your deductible, discounts and coverage. You don't need to wait for your renewal date to switch.

[[genie]]

## Why your rate went up

Rates can rise even with a clean record. Repair costs, medical costs and claims in your area all affect prices. Your own changes matter too: a ticket or accident, a new car, a teen driver, a move, or a lapse in coverage. Ask your insurer which applies to you. If it's a mistake on your record, you can get it fixed.

## 10 ways to lower your car insurance

Average savings from one 2026 analysis ([CarInsurance.com](https://www.carinsurance.com/how-to-lower-car-insurance)):

| Step | Typical savings |
|---|---|
| 1. Shop around and switch | About $694 a year |
| 2. Raise your deductible | 15% to 30% for a moderate increase |
| 3. Pay in full instead of monthly | About 9% |
| 4. Bundle with home insurance | About 13% (6% with renters) |
| 5. Join a safe-driving (telematics) program | About 10% |
| 6. Low-mileage discount (under 7,500 miles a year) | About 9% |
| 7. Good-driver discount | Up to 17% |
| 8. Multi-car discount | Often around 12%, up to 25% at some insurers |
| 9. Go paperless | About 3% |
| 10. Drop collision and comprehensive on an old car | Can be over $1,500 a year, but you lose cover for your own car |

### 1. Shop around
Get at least three quotes with the **same limits and deductibles**, so you compare like for like. Our guide on [how to compare car insurance quotes](/blog/how-to-compare-car-insurance-quotes/) shows how.

### 2. Raise your deductible
A higher deductible means a lower premium, but more out of pocket after a claim. Only raise it to an amount you could pay tomorrow.

### 3. Ask for every discount
Paying in full, paperless billing, safe-driver, low-mileage and multi-car discounts often aren't applied automatically. Call and ask.

### 4. Rethink full coverage on an older car
If your car is worth little and paid off, collision and comprehensive may cost more than they'd ever pay out. Lenders require them on financed cars.

### 5. Keep your coverage continuous
Never let your policy lapse to save money in the short term. A gap usually raises your next price. If money is tight, see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/).

If you started driving for Uber, Lyft or a delivery app, tell your insurer and ask about a [rideshare add-on](/rideshare-insurance/); an undisclosed rideshare claim can cost far more than the add-on.

## Common questions

### How can I lower my car insurance after a rate increase?
Compare quotes from several insurers first. Then raise your deductible, ask for discounts and review your coverage ([CarInsurance.com](https://www.carinsurance.com/how-to-lower-car-insurance)).

### Can I switch car insurance before my renewal?
Yes. You can switch at any time. Start the new policy before cancelling the old one so there's no gap, and ask about any refund of unused premium.

### Does raising my deductible really save money?
Usually. A moderate increase can cut 15% to 30% off the premium (same source), but you'll pay more yourself if you make a claim.

### Why did my insurance go up with no accidents?
Prices also rise with repair and medical costs, more claims in your area, or changes such as a new car or driver. Ask your insurer for the reason.

### How can I prevent my insurance going up after an accident?
Accident forgiveness can stop your first at-fault accident raising your price, if your insurer offers it and you qualify. See [comparing accident forgiveness](/blog/how-to-compare-car-insurance-quotes/) and our guide to [insurance after a ticket or accident](/car-insurance-after-accident-or-ticket/).
