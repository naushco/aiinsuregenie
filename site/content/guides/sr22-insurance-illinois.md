**Quick answer:** an SR-22 in Illinois is a form your insurer files with the state Secretary of State to prove you carry at least Illinois's minimum liability coverage (25/50/20). You usually need it for three years. With an SR-22, the average Illinois driver pays about **$100 a month for liability** and **$196 for full coverage**, compared with $83 and $162 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/illinois/sr-22-car-insurance)).

## Who needs an SR-22 in Illinois

You'll typically be told to file one after ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/illinois/sr-22-car-insurance)):

- Causing an accident while uninsured
- Not paying a court judgment from an accident
- A license revocation
- Three or more convictions for driving without insurance

The court or Secretary of State notice tells you if you need one and for how long. See our [complete SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/) for how SR-22s work in general.

## Minimum coverage with an SR-22 in Illinois

Your policy must carry at least Illinois's minimum liability limits:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $25,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $50,000 |
| Property damage | $20,000 |

Illinois also requires uninsured motorist coverage of $25,000 / $50,000 on SR-22 policies. Processing can take up to 30 days, so file early.

## How long you need it

Usually **three years**. You must keep coverage active for the whole period, with no gaps.

## What SR-22 insurance costs in Illinois

The SR-22 filing fee is set by your insurer (Illinois doesn't publish one). The bigger cost is the higher premium. Lowest average monthly prices for Illinois drivers with an SR-22 in a 2026 analysis ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/illinois/sr-22-car-insurance)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-Owners | $50 | $88 |
| State Farm | $54 | $97 |
| Certainly | $73 | $201 |
| Country Financial | $75 | $134 |
| Progressive | $76 | $139 |
| Direct Auto | $77 | $161 |

These are averages for sample drivers, not quotes. Your price depends on why you need the SR-22, your record, car and ZIP code, so compare several insurers.

## If you don't own a car

Illinois doesn't require a non-owner policy, but it's a common way to meet an SR-22 requirement if you don't own a car. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).

## If your SR-22 lapses

If your SR-22 lapses or is cancelled, your insurer must tell the state, and the Secretary of State suspends your license until you file a new SR-22. Set up autopay, and see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/) if money is tight.

## Common questions

### How much is SR-22 insurance in Illinois?
About $100 a month for liability and $196 for full coverage on average, with the cheapest insurers from about $50 a month for liability ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/illinois/sr-22-car-insurance)).

### How long do you need an SR-22 in Illinois?
Usually **three years**.

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in Illinois?
In one 2026 study, Auto-Owners ($50 a month for liability), State Farm ($54) and Certainly ($73) were cheapest ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/illinois/sr-22-car-insurance)).

### Can I get an SR-22 in Illinois without a car?
Yes, usually with a non-owner policy that includes the SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
