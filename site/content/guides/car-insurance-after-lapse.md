**Quick answer:** if your car insurance lapsed, buy a new policy (or ask your old insurer to reinstate) as soon as possible, and don't drive until you're covered. The shorter the gap, the smaller the price increase.

A lapse in car insurance happens to a lot of drivers, and you can fix it. The most important thing is to **get covered again quickly**. A 2026 analysis found rates rose about **8%** on average after a lapse of 30 days or less, but about **35%** after a lapse longer than 30 days ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).

## What counts as a lapse

A lapse is any time your car is registered but not insured, even for one day ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)). Common causes:

- A missed payment that led to cancellation
- A renewal you forgot about
- Selling a car and not buying a new policy right away
- Stopping driving for a while without pausing coverage properly

## What can happen after a lapse

- **Higher rates.** Insurers see a gap as higher risk, and longer gaps cost more ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).
- **State penalties.** Insurers report cancellations to the state, which can mean fines, a suspended license or registration, or an SR-22 requirement. In Florida, for example, insurers must report a cancellation within 10 days, and reinstatement costs $150 the first time, $250 the second, and $500 for later ones within three years ([Florida Statutes s. 324.0221](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/324.0221)).
- **Fewer choices.** Some insurers won't quote drivers with a recent gap; others specialize in it ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).

## How to get covered again

1. **Call your old insurer first.** For short lapses, some will reinstate your policy with little penalty ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).
2. **Don't wait.** The penalty grows the longer you're uninsured.
3. **Be honest about the gap.** Insurers can see it in records, and hiding it can get a policy cancelled ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).
4. **Compare several insurers.** Some barely penalize short lapses, while others raise rates sharply.
5. **Check your state's rules.** If you got a notice, you may need to show proof of insurance or file an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/).

## How to keep it from happening again

- Turn on automatic payments
- Ask about a grace period for late payments
- If you're not driving for a while, ask about pausing coverage instead of cancelling
- Don't cancel your old policy until the new one is in place ([Texas Department of Insurance](https://tdi.texas.gov/tips/car-insurance.html))

## Common questions

### What happens if my car insurance lapses?
Your insurer may report the cancellation to the state, which can lead to fines or a suspended license or registration. Your next policy will also usually cost more, about 8% more after a gap of up to 30 days and about 35% more after a longer gap ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates); [Florida Statutes s. 324.0221](https://flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/324.0221)).

### My car insurance lapsed. What do I do now?
Call your old insurer and ask whether it can reinstate the policy. If not, compare a few insurers and buy a new policy right away. If you received a notice from your state, follow it, since you may need to show proof of insurance or file an [SR-22](/sr22-insurance/).

### How do I get proof of insurance right away after a lapse?
Most insurers let you download a digital ID card as soon as you buy a policy, so you can usually have proof the same day. If your state needs a filing such as an SR-22, ask the insurer to file it when you buy.

### Is there temporary or short-term car insurance after a lapse?
True short-term car insurance is rare in the US. Most insurers sell six-month or 12-month policies. If you only need coverage for a short time, ask whether you can cancel early and get a refund for the unused months, and whether there is a cancellation fee.

### How long can I go without car insurance before rates go up?
Some insurers don't penalize gaps under about two weeks. After 30 days, the average increase is much larger ([ValuePenguin](https://www.valuepenguin.com/how-does-lapse-car-insurance-coverage-affect-rates)).

### Will a lapse stay on my record?
Insurers typically look at whether you were continuously insured in recent years, so each month of coverage after the gap helps.

### Can I get insurance the same day after a lapse?
Usually yes. Many insurers can start a policy the same day, even after a gap.
