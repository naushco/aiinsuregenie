**Quick answer:** an SR-22 in California is a form your insurer files with the state DMV to prove you carry at least California's minimum liability coverage (30/60/15). You usually need it for three to five years. With an SR-22, the average California driver pays about **$112 a month for liability** and **$234 for full coverage**, compared with $98 and $204 for a clean record ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/california/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

## Who needs an SR-22 in California

You'll typically be told to file one after ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/california/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

- A DUI conviction
- Reckless driving
- Driving without insurance
- Too many moving violations or an at-fault accident without insurance

The court or DMV notice tells you if you need one and for how long. See our [complete SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/) for how SR-22s work in general.

## Minimum coverage with an SR-22 in California

Your policy must carry at least California's minimum liability limits:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Bodily injury, one person | $30,000 |
| Bodily injury, per accident | $60,000 |
| Property damage | $15,000 |


## How long you need it

Usually **three years**, and up to five years after more than one DUI. You must keep coverage active for the whole period, with no gaps.

## What SR-22 insurance costs in California

The SR-22 filing fee is $25 to $50. The bigger cost is the higher premium. Lowest average monthly prices for California drivers with an SR-22 in a 2026 analysis ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/california/sr-22-car-insurance/)):

| Insurer | Liability only | Full coverage |
|---|---|---|
| USAA (military families only) | $77 | $173 |
| Aspire General | $79 | $141 |
| GEICO | $79 | $162 |
| Sun Coast | $83 | $197 |
| Mercury | $84 | $166 |
| Progressive | $86 | $169 |

These are averages for sample drivers, not quotes. Your price depends on why you need the SR-22, your record, car and ZIP code, so compare several insurers.

## If you don't own a car

Not every insurer sells non-owner policies with SR-22 filings, so you may need to call around. A related Insurify analysis puts non-owner coverage at about $47 a month on average. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).

## If your SR-22 lapses

If your policy lapses or is cancelled, your insurer notifies the DMV, which can suspend your license again until a new SR-22 is filed. Set up autopay, and see [what to do about a late payment](/late-car-insurance-payment/) if money is tight.

## Common questions

### How much is SR-22 insurance in California?
About $112 a month for liability and $234 for full coverage on average, with the cheapest insurers from about $77 a month for liability ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/california/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### How long do you need an SR-22 in California?
Usually **three years**, and up to five years after more than one DUI.

### Who has the cheapest SR-22 insurance in California?
In one 2026 study, USAA ($77 a month for liability), Aspire General ($79) and GEICO ($79) were cheapest ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/california/sr-22-car-insurance/)).

### Can I get an SR-22 in California without a car?
Yes, usually with a non-owner policy that includes the SR-22 filing. See our [non-owner SR-22 guide](/sr22-insurance/non-owner/).
