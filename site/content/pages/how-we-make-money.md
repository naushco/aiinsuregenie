AI InsureGenie is free for drivers. We earn money from our quoting partners, not from you. This page explains how, so you can judge our suggestions with that in mind.

## Who pays us

- **Quoting partners pay us.** When you visit a partner from our site or chat, or request a quote from them, that partner may pay us a fee.
- **Drivers never pay us.** Using Genie, our guides and our tools costs nothing, and using them does not add anything to the price an insurer quotes you.

## How it can affect what you see

- **We only show partners that fit what you told us.** Genie narrows partners by your state and by what matters most to you, such as price, coverage or talking to an agent.
- **Our business arrangements can affect which partner is shown first.** Among the partners that fit, the one shown as the main option can depend on our agreements with partners. Other partners that fit are listed as other options.
- **Payment does not change our guides.** Facts in our guides come from state agencies, laws and established insurance sources, and are linked next to each claim. See [how we check facts](/how-we-check-facts/).

## What we are and are not

- AI InsureGenie is an AI assistant. We are **not an insurance company or a licensed insurance agent**, and we don't sell insurance.
- Quotes, prices and coverage come from the partner or insurer. Always read the policy before you buy.
- Our guides are general information, not insurance, legal or financial advice.

## Your information

We only share your contact details with a partner when you agree to be contacted. You can ask us not to sell or share your personal information on our [Do Not Sell or Share page](/do-not-sell/). Our [privacy policy](/privacy/) has the details.
